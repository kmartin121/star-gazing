5mm Field (150x)
1. Saturn & System (Bookend Views) Aquarius (SE → SSW) • 10mm (75x) & 5mm (150x)
First telescopic look; razor-sharp warm yellow-gold globe and ring plane. Rings appeared nearly edge-on with front dipping slightly down and back tilted up. Observed thin dark ring shadow across the globe, soft starburst halo, and subtle 4-point spikes. Resolved distant bright pinpoint of Titan plus an inner moon (Rhea/Tethys) nestled close. Timed field drift: 50 seconds center-to-edge at 150x. Returned near 4:00 AM for a final look.
20mm Field (37.5x)
2. The Pleiades (Messier 45) Taurus (East) • Naked Eye, 10mm, 20mm
Naked-eye view presented as a delicate, shimmering unresolved fuzzy patch. 10mm required panning across the field, while 20mm framed the entire cluster like a jewel box. Counted 8–9 prominent primary stars: 6 forming a distinct "mini dipper" bowl with 2 outliers marking the handle/edge. Unmatched contrast between brilliant icy blue-white anchors and delicate pinpoints over a flat, jet-black background (no nebulosity detected).
10mm Field (75x)
3. Aldebaran (Alpha Tauri) Taurus (East) • 20mm, 5mm, 10mm
The "Eye of the Bull" appeared as a blazing, molten golden nugget in the 20mm. Switching to 5mm produced edge hazing and a dark annular blackout ring (exit pupil/eye relief boundary). The 10mm delivered the sweet spot: deep golden hue with prominent 4-point diffraction cross spikes. Noted the lower vertical spike was noticeably thicker than the rest—caused by tube boundary layer thermal currents and low-altitude atmospheric dispersion.
5mm Field (150x)
4. Great Orion Nebula (Messier 42) & Sword Orion (East / SE) • Naked Eye, 20mm, 5mm (150x)
Viewed Belt naked-eye to appreciate its full 3° span. In the sword, M42 started as a faint smudge in the 20mm before blooming into dramatic structure at 5mm. The cloud displayed a distinctive swept single-wing fan shape with a moody dark navy tone. Cleanly split all 4 stars of the central Trapezium cluster. Traced a prominent line of three stars extending out from the top edge of the cloud toward the sword tip.
20mm Field (37.5x)
5. Alnitak Field (Horsehead Region / Barnard 33 Attempt) Orion's Belt • 20mm (37.5x) & 10mm (75x)
Scanned the eastern Belt region off Alnitak attempting visual detection of the Horsehead Nebula. No dark silhouette or faint emission mist was resolved due to Alnitak's intense glare and human eye insensitivity to faint H-alpha wavelengths. Instead, resolved a stunning, dense "sheet of stars" filling the background field. Excellent lesson in visual limits vs. long-exposure astrophotography.
