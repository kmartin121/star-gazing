Optics / Eyepieces 150mm Dob (20mm, 10mm, 5mm)
Eyepiece Field
1. Venus (Crescent Phase) Virgo (Low West) • 10mm (75x) & 5mm (150x)
First object spotted in dusk sky. Appears like a thick fingernail crescent. 10mm gave a defined, sharp view; 5mm magnified noticeable atmospheric "boiling" near horizon. Warm orange body with subtle blue prismatic dispersion fringing along the lower cusp.
Eyepiece Field
2. Arcturus (Alpha Boötis) Boötes (West) • 10mm (75x)
First star out as night set in. Shines like a diamond with steady sparkle. Color perception split: rich yellow-gold vs. brilliant white core. Distinct circular core with a spotted outer Airy ring. Prominent 4-point secondary diffraction spikes radiating across the field.
Eyepiece Field
3. Vega (Alpha Lyrae) Lyra (Zenith) • 10mm (75x)
Located directly overhead. Icy, cool blue-white point source with long cross spikes. Outer edge showed slight blue fringing on top, yellow on bottom. Encountered the classic Dobsonian "zenith hole"—extreme altitude angle made manual tracking and centering challenging.
Eyepiece Field
4. ISS (Zarya Module Flyover) Overhead Pass (17,500 mph) • 25mm Widefield
High-speed tandem manual tracking: one steered the Dob while the other observed in 1–2 second bursts! Blazing pure white. Distinct "chunky cross / H-shape" clearly visible (central module spine flanked by solar panel arrays). Vanished mid-sky into Earth's orbital shadow.
Eyepiece Field
5. The Double Cluster (NGC 869 & NGC 884) Perseus (Northeast) • 10mm (75x) & 20mm (37.5x)
Sprinkling of diamond-dust stars. Lower cluster is visibly denser with two prominent companion stars close together; upper cluster is more open with stars further apart. A loose third grouping floats between them. Spotted flickering red supergiant stars! 20mm framed both clusters comfortably. Multiple shooting stars seen overhead.
